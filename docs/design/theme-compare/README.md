# Theme comparison shots (D17)

These are the Glass versus Flat comparison shots of 2026-11-07 that the owner chose from. The owner chose Flat (D17, T-485).

The theme lab that made them (`/[locale]/theme-lab`, its Playwright spec and its fixtures) was removed in commit `1d82c29`, together with the Glass stylesheet import in `a7b6135`. They are not kept up to date, and nothing in the product reads them: the Glass shots show a look that no longer exists in the code, and the Flat shots show the product as it was on that day.
